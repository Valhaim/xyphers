Scope
  Both are exact finite constructions, not fitted simulations. No random
  seed, numerical tolerance or trajectory length chooses the temperature.

  CAL-ALPHA-0 shows that, in its declared family, the reservoir relation is
  the operational temperature and the receipt formula is not an equation of
  state. It does not establish a network-size scaling law, THAIM as heat or
  work, or a joule calibration.

  CAL-ENDO-1 shows that adaptive digital graphs can build and repair the
  energy-multiplicity relation that grounds their own temperature. Its
  alphabet, construction grammar, energy quantum and lawful rewrites are
  designed rather than discovered, and the grammar fixes b. Repair is a
  finite driven episode drawing on a named work store. The future-entropy
  readout Delta S_tau is not identified with the state-count entropy used
  here, and THAIM and Xi are not instantiated. The next question, stated in
  the result, is whether generic adaptive dynamics select such a grammar
  without having it installed.
