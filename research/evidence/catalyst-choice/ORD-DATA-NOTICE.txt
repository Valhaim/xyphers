Adaptation and modifications
  The Xyphers CHEM-V9/CHEM-V10 artifacts in this packet are modified extracts
  and analyses of that ORD object. The preparation stripped outcome payloads
  before decoding the target-blind freeze; selected ligand-loaded rows;
  canonicalized ligand identities; constructed recipe blocks, cohorts,
  molecular descriptors, plate-layout audits, and permutation strata; then,
  after the freeze, extracted desired-product percentage yields and calculated
  the published statistics and numerical audits. CHEM-V10 changed only the
  required-slot coverage rule described in its preregistration and manifest.

  These modifications were made by the Xyphers research project. They are not
  endorsed by the Open Reaction Database Project or the source-study authors.
